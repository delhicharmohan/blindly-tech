<h1>About Blindly</h1>
<p class="lede">Fall for the person, not the profile.</p>

<div class="content" markdown="1">

[Blindly](https://blindly.date/) is a blind dating app in India designed for real, personality-first connections. There are no photos and no bios. You pick your mood, connect with people looking for the same vibe, and stay anonymous until you both choose to reveal.

## How Blindly works

1. **Don't judge.** Your profile starts with who you are, not how you look. Nothing to swipe on.
2. **Choose your mood.** Coffee or dinner, a long drive, Netflix and chill, deep talks.
3. **Speak.** Record 30 seconds in your own voice and talk with people in the same mood.
4. **Match the vibe.** Voice notes and chats, fully anonymous on both sides.
5. **Reveal.** Faces stay hidden until you both choose to reveal. One yes isn't enough.

## Safety

- Profiles are verified with a liveness check to stop fake accounts.
- Your identity stays hidden until a mutual reveal.
- Screen recording in the app is detected, and leaked videos can be traced back to the account they came from (in beta).
- You can report anyone from their profile or chat. More on the [safety page](https://blindly.date/safety).

## Availability

Blindly is free and open to adults aged 18 and over across India. It is launching on the App Store and Google Play. [Join the waitlist](https://blindly.date/) for early access.
