Welcome to **Blindly Engineering**, the tech blog of [Blindly](https://blindly.date/).

Blindly is a blind dating app in India. No photos, no bios: you choose your mood, talk by voice, and reveal each other only when you both say yes. Building that takes some interesting engineering, and this is where we'll write about it.

## What we're building

Most dating apps are built around a photo and a swipe. Blindly is built around a voice and a mood:

- **Mood-based matching.** You tell us what you're up for right now (coffee or dinner, a long drive, Netflix and chill, deep talks), and we connect you with people feeling the same.
- **Voice first.** Your intro is 30 seconds in your own voice. Conversations happen through voice notes and private chat.
- **Mutual reveal.** Faces stay hidden until both people choose to reveal. One yes isn't enough.
- **Safety by design.** Liveness checks to stop fake accounts, screen-recording detection, and leak tracing that can link a leaked video back to the account it came from (in beta).
